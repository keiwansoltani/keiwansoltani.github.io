RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Scientific ML for electromagnetic-system modeling

I am interested in extending data-driven physical-property prediction, neural-network architecture optimization, and constrained optimization to electromagnetic-system modeling and design. Prospective applications include signal integrity, power integrity, electromagnetic compatibility, and electronic design automation, in collaboration with domain specialists using simulated and measured engineering data.
